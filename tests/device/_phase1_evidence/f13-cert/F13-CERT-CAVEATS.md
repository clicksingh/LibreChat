# F13-CERT-CAVEATS — what this certification does **not** establish

Companion to `F13-CERT-REPORT.md` (Part B).

1. **One interleaving per round, not adversarial scheduling.** Both children are released by the same file barrier, but the order in which they reach the database is whatever the OS schedules. The window is barrier-aligned, not widened or narrowed on purpose, and no round sweeps a range of offsets between the two calls.
2. **The issuance leg targets a `claim_pending` session, not an `open` one.** Role A issues while the round's armed session is `claim_pending`; the law's other half ("issuance **may** supersede a `state:open` session") is therefore only observed indirectly (the round's own `open` session created by arming is superseded by design at arm time). A supersede of a genuinely `open` session is exercised by the product's normal arming path, not by a race here.
3. **Roles are not symmetric.** Only role A issues and only role B approves; there is no round where both attempt the same operation, and no round where the approval is issued from a second owner.
4. **`N ≥ 50` is met by 25 rounds × 2 attempts**, which is 50 attempts but 25 independent sessions. It is not 50 independent device rows (the existing V24-T15 pattern is 25 rows for the same reason: `uq_owner_active` allows one live lease per account).
5. **The F13 fence is evaluated, not the whole pairing protocol.** Proof verification, consent binding, challenge TTLs and the audit sink are the harness's disclosed seams (`verifyLocalConsent` echoes the trusted session truth; the device key is a deterministic lab key). The certification covers the `sessionVersion` fence and the session/authority transitions only.
6. **The certificate pins bytes, not a deployment.** `F13-CERT-RUN-META.json` records HEAD and the SHA-256 of the 7 product files that were loaded; the surrounding worktree was dirty with **another lane's unrelated edits** (`api/server/routes/devices.js`, `client/src/routes/index.tsx`, `api/server/services/Device/{deviceCapabilityPolicy,currentDeviceRenewEnvelopeHandler}.js`, `relay/`). Those bytes were not exercised by these fixtures, but a later reader must not assume the whole worktree equals HEAD.
7. **`EXPECTED_RED` is reported, not endorsed.** The existing `T0A-01` row's status and its hardcoded pre-repair narrative are reproduced verbatim in the artifact because editing an existing fixture is out of scope; §2 of the report states plainly that the row does not evaluate the F13 oracle.
8. **The `E2-V24T15` flake is not root-caused here.** §5 of the report records the failing rounds and the exact closed failure mode, plus three immediate standalone passes. No timing instrumentation was added to the fixture to explain *why* the arming call landed before the revoke in those two rounds, and no attempt was made to reproduce it deterministically.
9. **No HTTP surface is certified.** Every attempt drives the service factories directly (`createPairingSessionService` / `createAuthorityService` over the frozen model registry). Routes, auth middleware and the handlers added in the recent commits are out of scope for this artifact.
10. **The namespace evidence is a snapshot pair.** `F13-CERT-NAMESPACE-BEFORE.json` / `…-AFTER.json` bracket this runner's own work; they cannot prove that a concurrent process did not create and drop a database entirely between the two snapshots.
